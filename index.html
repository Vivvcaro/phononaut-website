Hundreds of people have experienced it worldwide

Phononaut emerged from founder and CEO Viviana Caro’s PhD research into sound, ritual and consciousness, and her personal experience of navigating mental health challenges through music.

Her methodology, Acoustic States of Consciousness, explores altered states through sound and has been shared with hundreds of people worldwide.

Founder

Viviana Caro

Long before I had the words for it, I used sound to ‘trip’ naturally, to shift my perception and explore my inner world.

After my PTSD diagnosis, this became more intentional. Therapy was hard to access and didn’t always feel like enough. Psychedelics were life-changing, but could also be frightening. Music saved my life.

My background in neuroaesthetics, psychology and sound art helped me understand these experiences and shaped my research into how sound can support the exploration of altered states without drugs.

Phononaut is my way of making sound experiences informed by research more accessible, with the hope of helping others find connection, possibility and some relief from suffering.
