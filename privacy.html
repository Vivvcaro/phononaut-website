Who we are

Phononaut is made by Phantom Fish Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 17591944. If you have a question about your data, write to hello@phononaut.com.

What we collect

When you create an account: your name and email address. If you choose to add them: your age, gender and occupation. When you use Phononaut: the journeys you take, the intentions you set, and the reflections and answers you give before and after each journey, written or spoken.

To decide: whether spoken answers are kept as recordings or only as text.

Why we collect it

To run your account and the app, to keep your reflections in My dives for you, and, with your consent, for Viviana Caro’s research into how sound shapes altered states of consciousness.

To decide: whether research use is required to use the app or something you can opt in to, and what the research ethics approval allows.

Research use

What reaches the research does not carry your name. [How answers are de-identified, who can see them and how long they are kept.]

To decide: who can see reflections, and whether answers from Live sessions join your account.

Where your data is kept

Accounts and answers are stored with our providers, Supabase and Cloudflare R2. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

To confirm: the storage regions and the providers’ data agreements.

How long we keep it

[For as long as you have an account, and for research data, as set out in the ethics approval.]

To decide: retention periods.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can see the data we hold about you, correct it, download it, or ask us to delete it. In the app, Privacy lets you download your data or delete your account. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

To decide: what deleting your account removes, including answers already shared for research.

Children

Phononaut is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
